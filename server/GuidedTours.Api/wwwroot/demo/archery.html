Target archery

The Olympic discipline. Shoot at a 122 cm face from fixed distances on a flat, measured range.

  • Distances 18 m indoors, 30 to 70 m outdoors
  • Recurve, compound or longbow

Frequently asked questions

Can't find an answer? Email the club secretary.

Do I need my own equipment?

No. Beginners use club kit for the whole course. Most members buy their own bow after a few months.

Is there a minimum age?

Juniors can join from age 8. Under 12s must shoot with a parent or guardian on the range.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain doesn't stop us. In high wind or lightning the range officer closes the range, and the status bar at the top of this page updates.

Can I try before I join?

Yes. Book a one-off taster on any range session using the "Book a range session" button.
